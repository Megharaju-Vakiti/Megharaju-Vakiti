## 🚀 Featured Portfolio Projects

### 1. 📉 [Telco Customer Churn & Retention Analytics Dashboard](https://github.com/Megharaju-Vakiti/customer-churn-analysis)
> **An executive subscription retention solution evaluating 7,043 enterprise accounts and $1.67M USD in annualized revenue at risk.**

- **Domain:** Telecommunications & Subscription Economics &bull; **Tools:** Power BI, DAX Measures, Advanced Excel
- **Core Insights:** Diagnosed a baseline **26.54% churn rate** ($139.1K MRR loss). Isolated **Month-to-Month contracts** as the primary risk driver (42.7% churn vs. 2.8% for 2-year contracts — a 15x risk delta). Identified the first 12 months as the highest attrition window (64.2% of all churn).
- **Deliverables:** Star-schema relational model, dynamic DAX measures (Retention Rate, Churn Rate %, ARR at Risk), drill-through cohorts, and an actionable retention playbook.
- [🔗 View Repository](https://github.com/Megharaju-Vakiti/customer-churn-analysis)

### 2. 🛒 [Omnichannel Retail Business Performance & MIS Reporting System](https://github.com/Megharaju-Vakiti/Retail_business_performance_analysis)

- **Domain:** E-Commerce, Retail & Logistics &bull; **Tools:** MySQL, Power BI, Advanced Excel, Power Query
- **Core Insights:** Evaluated 11,965+ transaction logs and 3,525+ customer profiles across 8 product categories and 10 fulfillment channels. Wrote complex SQL queries utilizing multi-table JOINs, subqueries, and window functions to assess product margins and regional demand.
- **Key Impact:** Established data reconciliation checks between raw transactional tables and Excel summary sheets, maintaining **98%+ data accuracy** and **reducing manual report preparation time by ~25%**.
- [🔗 View Repository](https://github.com/Megharaju-Vakiti/Retail_business_performance_analysis)

- **Domain:** Automotive & Electric Vehicle Transition &bull; **Tools:** Python (Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn), Statsmodels, Power BI
- **Core Insights:** Tracked electrification transition (BEV + PHEV surging from 7.1% to 28.5% market share). Conducted time-series decomposition to isolate quarterly seasonality and generated demand forecasts with confidence intervals using ARIMA modeling.
- **Deliverables:** Automated Python EDA scripts, statistical distributions, correlation matrices, and an interactive executive Power BI dashboard.
- [🔗 View Repository](https://github.com/Megharaju-Vakiti/bmw-global-sales-analysis)
